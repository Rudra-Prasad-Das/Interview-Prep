# Interview-Prep
Primarily for the Culture Fit rounds
# Discuss a time when you had to deal with a tough colleague

This question can have several possible variations like,
Discuss a time when you had a clash of opinions with your colleague.
Points :

Situation : 

Had misalignment in opinions in the design of a product [ Multi Capture Shot Suggestion Project ].
Colleague wanted to add pre-existing AI models for a safe launch, not interested in exploring new AI models.
I was in opinion of exploring a using out home grown AI models with our own datasets.

Task:

Needed to convince that using pre-existing AI models woould not give us improved results and there would be little to 
no novelty in the product results. Using pre-existing open source models would be difficult to get approved as the 
emphasis is always given to self trained and thouroughly tested AI-models.

Action: 

Tried to be as respectful to my colleague as possible and win his trust.Actively listened to all his points and asked for clarifications.
Invited members/mentors who were not actively contributing to the projects but were a part of it for their unbiased and valuable opinion.
Waited for my turn and explained with prior data and evidences of the suggested models as why they were not suitable for our use-case.
The other open-source AI models were also not suitable as they were trained and designed for a different product and use-case and we would not 
get the desired result with them.

Result.

All the team members and mentors weighed all the points given as input in the discussions and were collectively able to reach an agreement.
My colleague felt that his opinions were taken care off and well addressed, and was convinced why proceeding with his approach would not have given results.
